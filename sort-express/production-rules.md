# Sort Express — Production Rules

> **Purpose:** Hard constraints that cause rejection if violated.
> **Last Updated:** 2026-02-04

Hard constraints for Sort Express production. These rules define the non-negotiable requirements for the game.

---

## Visual Quality Standards

**High-Quality Assets Required**
- All items and general images must be in high quality
- Items should have a 3D look-alike, rounded and very polished appearance matching the game style
- Items must be polished, rounded 3D look-alike (AI generation allowed if matching game vibe)

**Proportional Design**
- Proportion of items, characters, and assets must be proportional to the rest of the scene
- This requirement exists to avoid frequent playable fixes

## Core Gameplay Mechanics

**Match-3 System**
- Primary action is sorting and matching 3D in-game items on shelves
- Goal is to match 3 identical objects to clear them from the shelf
- Items can be moved between shelves if there is space

**Gameplay Variants**
- Gameplay involves matching 2, 3, or up to 5 items depending on the ideation

**Win/Lose Conditions**
- Win: All items on shelves are successfully matched and cleared
- Lose: Shelves become too full to move items, or time/move limits are reached

## Mandatory Ad Elements

**Close-Up Item Views**
- Gameplay ads must show in-game items up close (e.g., 8x3 layouts)
- This is required for stronger ad performance

**Dynamic Elements**
- Must include concepts like breaking shelves or moving shelves to add "glow" to original gameplay

## Camera & Presentation

**Camera Angle**
- Side-on / Front-facing view of shelves is required

## Art Direction

**Visual Style**
- 3D Stylized art style (specifically a "3D game" unlike direct competitors)
- Tone must be Casual / Fun / Organized
