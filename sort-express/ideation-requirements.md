# Ideation Requirements

Engagement strategies, hooks, and creative direction for Sort Express playable ads.

---

## AI-Generated Assets and Concepts Are Encouraged

**The client has a high exploration level and actively encourages AI-generated concepts, characters, intros, and narrative hooks.**

AI generation is allowed for:
- Character designs and intros
- Thematic concepts and visual styles
- Narrative hooks and storylines
- In-game items and assets (as long as they match the polished 3D vibe of the original gameplay)

The client wants variety and fresh creative directions, not just standard sorting gameplay repeated.

## Use Misleading Narrative Hooks to Create Emotional Engagement

**Ads can and should use narrative framing like "Mom is angry about messy toys -- organize them!" to create emotional hooks beyond pure gameplay.**

Narrative hook examples:
- Mom upset about a messy playroom
- Kitchen chaos before guests arrive
- Closet disaster before an important event
- Any relatable domestic organization scenario with emotional stakes

These create relatable motivation for the target audience (35+ women) and drive engagement beyond abstract puzzle solving.

## Feature Breaking Shelves as the Signature Moment

**The breaking shelves mechanic is the standout creative element. Ad #99 is the best example and should be used as reference for this approach.**

Breaking shelves concept:
- Shelves crack and shatter when cleared or overloaded
- Dramatic visual effect creates a "wow" moment
- New shelf slides in to replace the broken one
- Creates surprise and differentiates from static sorting competitors

This is the signature mechanic that sets Sort Express apart in ads.

## Deliver Both Unique Ideation and Classic Gameplay with Theme

**The partner wants to see unique new ideation concepts alongside classic sorting gameplay presented with a fresh theme. Both types of creative are expected.**

Two creative tracks to deliver:
1. **Unique ideation:** Novel concepts, AI-generated hooks, unexpected themes, narrative-driven ads
2. **Classic gameplay with theme:** Standard shelf sorting mechanic but set in a fresh thematic context (e.g., new environment, different item types)

Do not only deliver one type. The partner expects both creative exploration and grounded gameplay concepts.

## Proven Theme: Dishwasher Loading

**The dishwasher loading/organizing theme is a proven high-performing concept. Ad #46 is the reference.**

Dishwasher concept details:
- Items are dishes, cups, and utensils
- Shelves become dishwasher racks
- Match identical dishes to load them properly
- Relatable domestic task that resonates with the 35+ women audience

This theme works because it connects sorting mechanics to a universal real-world task.

## Competitors to Study

**Direct competitors are in the Goods Sorting genre: Goods Puzzle Sort Challenge and Goods Sort Matching Puzzle. Study them for positioning but differentiate with 3D quality.**

Sort Express differentiates from these competitors through:
- 3D stylized visuals (competitors tend toward 2D)
- Higher polish and visual quality
- Dynamic shelf mechanics (breaking, moving)
- Narrative hooks and thematic variety
